Sources, methods and limitations

About this visualisation

Domain, audience and method

Domain
Pet health and insurance in Malaysia.
What
Survey evidence, published policy terms, selected claims and Selangor district context.
Why
Help pet owners compare cover, costs and evidence gaps.
Who
Pet owners in Malaysia, including first-time insurance readers.
How
Vega-Lite charts and maps; shared plan, claim and district selections; hover for details.

Limits

  • The Malaysia map gives geographic context only; survey findings are limited to the named study samples.
  • The insurance study is a convenience sample of 116 Selangor participants; it is not a Malaysian prevalence estimate.
  • The six Oyen cases are selected examples, not representative claims data or a ranking of common Malaysian illnesses.
Source register
  1. Koh and colleagues, “Knowledge, Attitudes, and Practices of Pet Owners Regarding Pet Insurance in Selangor, Malaysia,” Pets, 2026. Fieldwork July to October 2024; sample size 116; convenience sample.
  2. Oyen policy terms and MSIG Malaysia Pet Insurance, checked 8 October 2026.
  3. Oyen, six selected claim examples, published 28 September 2026.
  4. Department of Statistics Malaysia, Selangor district household incomes, 2024.
  5. Department of Statistics Malaysia administrative district GeoJSON and its Open Data License. The maps use the nine Selangor district features.
  6. Natural Earth, 1:110m country boundaries. Muted neighbouring land provides context; no national survey values are encoded.
  7. Government of Malaysia, location overview. Geographic context only.

Survey microdata and policy source records are summarized in the published CSV files; no individual-level responses are shown.
