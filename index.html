Sources and methods

How to read this story

Malaysia survey results, Selangor study findings, insurer prices and district income are separate sources. They are shown side by side for context, not treated as one linked dataset.

The Selangor pet-insurance study recruited 116 participants by convenience sampling in July–October 2024. The national emergency-fund survey reports 4,872 pet owners for the dedicated-buffer question.

Limits

  • District practice shares describe only people in the study sample, not district populations.
  • Price expectations and current premiums are not matched to the same people or time.
  • Published claim examples are selected cases and do not estimate typical reimbursements.
