How do Australians protect the health of their pets?

This starter page demonstrates the project data, the FIT3179 TopoJSON map pattern and the first visualisation building blocks. It is not the final assignment page yet.

Draft — source check: 16 September 2026

1 · Australians are increasingly pet owners

Start with the broader household picture before introducing insurance and claims.

Households with at least one pet

Source: Animal Medicines Australia, Pets in Australia 2025. Survey reference point: March 2025.

2 · Protection is uneven

The first insurance chart uses the AMA owner-reported participation field. It is a simple example to edit before building the advanced idioms.

Owners reporting pet insurance, by species

Source: Animal Medicines Australia, Pets in Australia 2025. This is owner-reported participation, not insurer market share.

Next charts to build: pet-type treemap, spending composition, dog-versus-cat insurance interval, health-protection heatmap and policy coverage matrix.

3 · Claims reveal the risk

PetSure figures describe claims among insured pets, not every Australian pet.

PetSure condition and treatment-cost starter

Source: PetSure Pet Health Monitor current public page. Use the wording “among PetSure-insured pets”.

4 · Geography of registration and care

These maps use official South Australian Dogs and Cats Online data as registration context. They do not represent insurance uptake.

Choropleth · registered-animal share

Colour encodes registered_rate_pct. Keep the exact rate in the tooltip.

Proportional symbols · recorded-animal totals

Circle size encodes total_animals. Points are geometry-derived council anchors, not animal locations.

Bin map · explicit registration classes

Classes: under 90%, 90% to under 95%, and 95% or higher.

Data note: AMA is the broad household source; PetSure is an insured-claims source; South Australian DACO is a registration context source. Read the source register before writing final claims.
